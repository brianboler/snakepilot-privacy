SnakePilot Privacy Policy

Applies to the SnakePilot Chrome extension, version 1.0.0 and later. Last updated 2026-10-18.

SnakePilot is an optional autopilot for the browser game slither.io. When you switch it on, a small neural network that ships inside the extension steers your own snake. Everything runs on your device.

Summary

  • SnakePilot does not send any data to the developer or to any third party. It has no servers, no analytics, no advertising and no accounts.
  • SnakePilot does not collect personal information, browsing history, or anything from websites other than the slither.io game page (at slither.io, or at slither.com/io, where slither.io sometimes redirects).
  • Data that SnakePilot stores stays in your browser's local extension storage until you delete it.

What is stored on your device

  • Settings: whether the autopilot, auto-respawn, learning and recording are switched on, the maximum number of lives per session, and whether you acknowledged the first-run notice.
  • Statistics: number of autopilot lives, survival times, best score and safety-layer intervention counts.
  • Life log: one entry for each life that the autopilot steered, and for each life you play while "Record game states for calibration" is switched on. An entry holds the start time, survival time, how the life ended, final score, maximum length, number of autopilot steps, how often the safety layer stepped in, the distance to and size of the snake that killed yours (used to calibrate the safety layer), the address of the game's script file and the model version. Lives you play yourself with recording switched off are not logged. Nothing is logged before you acknowledge the first-run notice.
  • Optional gameplay telemetry (only if you switch on "Learn from my games" or "Record game states for calibration", which become available after you acknowledge the first-run notice): the numeric game state around your snake (positions, sizes and headings of nearby snakes and food) and the autopilot's decisions. Recording also covers lives you play yourself while it is switched on. Recording keeps every game state for the first 10 seconds of a life, then short samples at growing intervals, and stores at most 12 MB of recorded game states per life, plus at most 12 MB of learning telemetry per life when "Learn from my games" is on; anything beyond that is not stored.
  • Locally fine-tuned models (only with learning switched on): adjusted weights of the shipped model, produced on your device.

None of this data includes your nickname, chat, IP address or any identifier of you or other players beyond the game's own numeric snake ids.

The life log, gameplay telemetry and locally fine-tuned models count toward a 50 MB local storage cap. When the cap is reached, the oldest life-log entries and their telemetry are deleted automatically; with learning on, the entries whose learning telemetry is still used for fine-tuning are kept longest (their recorded game states are deleted first). A life that never reported its end (for example because you reloaded or closed the page mid-game) is marked "abandoned" when that tab starts its next life: its learning telemetry is deleted, and its life-log entry and recorded game states are kept (and evicted like any other life), or the entry is deleted if nothing was stored for it. Settings and statistics take a few kilobytes and are not counted toward the cap.

What is sent off your device

Nothing. SnakePilot makes no network requests of its own. It reads the slither.io game page that you opened yourself (at slither.io or slither.com/io) and writes the mouse direction and boost state that the game already accepts from your mouse. It does not store the address of the page.

How to export or delete your data

  • Export: open the SnakePilot popup, section Data, and use Export lives (the life log) or Export recordings. The file is saved through your browser's normal download prompt.
  • Delete: popup, section Data, Clear all data. Uninstalling the extension also deletes all of its stored data.

Sale or transfer of data

SnakePilot does not sell, rent, share or transfer any user data, and does not use or transfer data for purposes unrelated to its single purpose (letting you switch on an on-device AI that steers your own snake), for creditworthiness or lending, or for advertising.

Permissions

  • storage: to keep the settings, statistics, life log and optional telemetry listed above.
  • Access to slither.io pages and to slither.com/io pages (content scripts): to read the game state and steer your snake while the autopilot is on. slither.com/io is included only because the slither.io server sometimes redirects a visit there, and it serves the same game. SnakePilot does not run on other pages of slither.com or on any other website.

Game rules

Automated play may violate the game's terms of service and can lead to your access being blocked. SnakePilot is off by default, shows this notice before first use, and does not try to hide itself from the game.

Contact

Questions about this policy: use the support link on the extension's Chrome Web Store page.
